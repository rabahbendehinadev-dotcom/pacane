Act as a senior ERP architect and full-stack engineer.

Implement a new structured production organization feature in Pacane ERP:

OUVRIERS / RESPONSABLES DE PRÉPARATION

The goal is to organize the automatic replenishment / bon de commande workflow by responsible worker.

==================================================
BUSINESS CONTEXT
==================================================

In this ERP, products can require preparation by specific workers.

Example:
- Product A is prepared by worker "Rabah"
- Product B is prepared by worker "Ghanou"
- Product C is prepared by worker "Rabah"

Today, the automatic bon de commande / replenishment page can calculate what needs to be produced or replenished.
But it shows all products together.

This is not operationally convenient.

I want to assign each product to one responsible worker, so that:
- products are organized by worker
- I can filter the automatic replenishment page by worker
- I can print a bon de commande / preparation sheet for one worker only
- each worker receives only the products they are responsible for

Important:
This worker assignment is GLOBAL for the product across the whole site/app.
For now, one product = one responsible worker.
Do NOT make it branch-specific in this first version.

==================================================
MAIN GOAL
==================================================

Build:
1. A new page/module: Ouvriers
2. A new field on products: responsible worker
3. Integration into the automatic replenishment / bon de commande page:
   - filter by worker
   - group by worker
   - print by worker
