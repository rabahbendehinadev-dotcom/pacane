==================================================
BUSINESS CONTEXT
==================================================

This ERP is multi-branch.

We have some internal consumable products such as:
- Frottoir
- Balai
- Chiffon
- Other cleaning / maintenance / internal-use items

These products:
- are NOT sold to external customers
- are consumed internally by our boutiques / branches
- are often stored in a central stock location such as "Ecole Stock"
- then sent internally to boutiques like BEN OMAR, SIDI YAHYA, etc.

Business need:
I want to move these internal consumables from a central stock branch (example: Ecole Stock) to one of my boutiques, and track how much each boutique consumes.

Important:
This must NOT be treated as a normal customer sale.
This must NOT increase customer sales revenue.
This must NOT appear in customer sales analytics.

However:
I still want the boutique to receive the product internally at the same purchase cost.

Example:
- I buy 1 frottoir for 100 DA
- it enters stock in ECOLE STOCK
- boutique BEN OMAR needs 1
- I issue an internal consumption / internal cession document
- stock decreases in ECOLE STOCK
- stock increases in BEN OMAR
- BEN OMAR is charged internally with cost = 100 DA
- this must be visible later in internal consumption reports by boutique

==================================================
CORE BUSINESS RULES
==================================================

1. This is NOT a customer sale
- no client
- no customer receivable
- no sales revenue
- no impact on sales KPIs
- no impact on POS
- no impact on external invoices

2. This is NOT just a basic transfer
A normal transfer only moves stock.
Here we also need internal cost attribution to the destination boutique.

3. Internal consumables must be tracked by boutique
I want to know:
- how much each boutique received
- how much internal consumable cost each boutique accumulated
- which products are consumed most internally
- per period / per branch / per product

4. Internal consumables should move at purchase cost
Default rule:
- use current purchase cost / standard cost / latest purchase cost already available in product cost logic
- do NOT use sales price
- do NOT generate margin
- internal transfer price = cost price
