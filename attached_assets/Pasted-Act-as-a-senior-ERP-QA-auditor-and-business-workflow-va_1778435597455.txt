==================================================
BUSINESS CONTEXT
==================================================

This module is used for internal consumable products such as:
- frottoir
- balai
- chiffon
- other internal-use supplies

These products:
- are stocked in a central branch such as ECOLE STOCK
- are sent internally to boutiques like BEN OMAR, SIDI YAHYA, etc.
- are NOT customer sales
- must NOT affect sales revenue
- must move at purchase cost / internal cost
- must allow tracking internal consumable cost by boutique

The expected workflow is:
- source branch stock decreases
- destination boutique stock increases
- internal cost is assigned to destination boutique
- this appears in dedicated internal consumption reports
- this does NOT appear as customer turnover

AUDIT CHECKLIST
==================================================

1. PRODUCT ELIGIBILITY
Verify:
- only products flagged as internal consumable can be selected

- product selector shows correct source-branch stock
- product unit is correct

2. SOURCE / DESTINATION BRANCH RULES
Verify:
- source branch is required
- destination branch is required

3. STOCK MOVEMENT LOGIC
Verify on confirmation:
- source stock decreases correctly
- destination stock increases correctly
- quantities are exact
- no negative stock allowed in source
- no double application if user clicks twice
- stock movement rows are created
- movement types are distinct and correct
- stock history remains coherent

4. COST LOGIC
Verify:
- unit cost comes from purchase/standard cost logic, not sales price
- total line cost = quantity × unit cost
- document total = sum of line totals
- cost remains stable after confirmation
- destination boutique internal cost is tracked correctly
- no margin/revenue is generated from this workflow

5. STATUS LOGIC
Verify statuses:
- draft
- confirmed
- cancelled

- draft does not move stock
- confirmed moves stock exactly once

- confirmed document cannot be re-confirmed and duplicate stock movement
- status badges and detail views are consistent
