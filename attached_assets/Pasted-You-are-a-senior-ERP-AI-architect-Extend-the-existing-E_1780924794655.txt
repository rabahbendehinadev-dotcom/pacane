## A. Demand Forecast Engine

Create function:

forecastProductDemand(productId, branchId, period)

Logic:
- Use historical sales data
- Apply weighted moving average
- Give more weight to recent data
- Consider day-of-week patterns
- Consider branch-specific behavior

Output:
- predicted daily demand
- weekly demand
- confidence score (0-100%)

==================================================

## B. Stock Risk Engine

Create function:

calculateStockRisk(productId, branchId)

Rules:
- If stock coverage < 2 days → HIGH RISK
- If 2–5 days → MEDIUM RISK
- If > 5 days → SAFE

Output:
- current coverage days
- risk level
- suggested reorder quantity

==================================================

## C. Automatic Purchase Suggestion Engine

Create function:

generatePurchaseSuggestions(branchId)

Logic:
- For each raw material:
  - calculate expected consumption from forecasted production
  - subtract current stock
  - compute required purchase quantity

Output:
- list of materials to buy
- quantity
- urgency level
- estimated cost (using latest purchase price)

==================================================

## D. Boutique Consumption Analytics

Create function:

analyzeBranchConsumption(branchId)

- consumption trends (up/down)
- anomalies detection (sudden spikes or drops)

## E. Waste / Overproduction Detection

Output:
